What We Do

Complete Car Care Solutions

Every service your car needs, delivered by certified technicians using premium products and advanced equipment — all under one roof in Thoraipakkam, Chennai.

Mechanical Repairs & Periodic Maintenance

Engine diagnostics, brake & suspension work, oil changes and scheduled maintenance to keep your car running at peak performance.

Body Work — Denting, Tinkering & Accident Repairs

Precision panel beating and structural repair that restores your car's body to factory specification after dents or accidents.

Paint Work & Paint Restoration

Colour-matched respraying and full paint restoration using baking booths for a factory-finish shine that lasts.

Car Detailing — Interior & Exterior

Deep interior sanitisation and exterior gloss enhancement that leaves your car looking and feeling brand new.

Ceramic Coating

Long-lasting hydrophobic ceramic protection that guards your paint against UV damage, oxidation and swirl marks.

Paint Protection Film (PPF)

Self-healing film that shields your paint from stone chips, scratches and road debris while keeping the factory look.

Paint Correction & Machine Polishing

Multi-stage machine polishing that removes swirl marks and imperfections, restoring deep gloss and clarity.

Premium Water Wash

Meticulous foam wash and rinse process using pH-balanced products that keep your car spotless without harming the paint.
